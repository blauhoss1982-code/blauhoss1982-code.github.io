Privacy policy — hermes-backup

Last updated: 20 November 2026

hermes-backup is a personal backup client. It is operated by its owner for the owner's own Google Account and no one else.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads files from the owner's computer and creates, updates and lists files inside the owner's own Google Drive account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). It does not read Gmail, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.

Where the data goes

Files are uploaded only to the owner's own Google Drive. No copy is sent to the app developer, to third parties, or to analytics or advertising services. The app does not sell, share or monetise any data.

Storage and retention

The app stores an OAuth token on the owner's computer so it can talk to Google Drive without asking for sign-in every time. File copies in Google Drive are retained according to the owner's own backup policy; the owner can delete them at any time in Google Drive.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the backup files in Google Drive directly.
